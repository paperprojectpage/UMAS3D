UMAS3D: Unified Mask-Free 3D Editing with Adaptive Source Supervision

Abstract

3D editing modifies existing 3D assets according to user intent while preserving unaffected geometry and appearance. Existing methods often rely on task-specific training, source-3D inversion or external 3D masks, and lack a unified paradigm for diverse editing conditions and tasks. We propose UMAS3D, a unified framework that is training-free, inversion-free and free from external 3D masks. UMAS3D unifies text, bounding boxes, reference images and their combinations within a single editing pipeline. We introduce Mask-Free Adaptive Region Modeling (MARM), which builds a coarse Edit/Keep prior from source-target visual differences and adaptively refines region assignments during target 3D generation. We further introduce Source-Guided Preservation Steering (SGPS), which steers Keep-region states to preserve source-consistent geometry and appearance while leaving Edit regions target-driven. Experiments show that UMAS3D supports addition, deletion, modification, appearance and pose editing while effectively preserving unedited source content. These advances establish UMAS3D as a general paradigm for flexible and source-preserving 3D editing across diverse editing conditions.
